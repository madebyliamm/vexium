Heroes 8

Radiant Center

hero-radiant-center

Dark bg with three slow-floating ambient orbs, centered headline with gradient-text accent, live badge with pulse dot, dual CTA, avatar-stack social proof. Best for SaaS, startups, modern products.

Product Split

hero-product-split

Dark two-column hero: headline/copy/CTAs on the left, a CSS-only browser-chrome mockup with fake product UI on the right, greyscale logo strip beneath. Best for SaaS/product-led tools.

Editorial Statement

hero-editorial

Light/cream background, type-driven layout with massive display headline, single accent word, near-zero decoration. Best for agencies, personal brands, bold consumer brands.

Grid Glow

hero-grid-glow

Dark hero with a subtle dot-grid texture and center glow, bold centered headline, and a horizontal metrics band beneath the CTA. Best for B2B/data products that want to lead with credibility.

Gradient Wash

hero-gradient-wash

Rich layered gradient background (deep indigo to near-black) with grain texture overlay, centered content, single bold CTA. Best for consumer apps and brands wanting an immersive, premium feel.

Glass Card

hero-glass-card

Dark ambient-glow background with content centered inside a frosted glass panel — ideal for waitlist/early-access hero or any hero needing an embedded form. Premium, modern feel.

Trust First

hero-trust-first

Light hero leading with credibility: rating badge above the headline, clear outcome-focused headline, greyscale logo strip below the fold. Best for B2B tools and marketplaces where trust drives conversion.

Bento Hero

hero-bento

Dark hero broken into an asymmetric bento-style grid: large headline cell plus supporting stat/feature cells of varying size. Breaks from the standard centered pattern — best for product-forward, modern brands.

Feature Sections 5

Bento Grid

feat-bento-grid

Dark asymmetric CSS grid — one large anchor cell with a mini product visual, surrounding cells of varying sizes with icon+title+description. The antidote to three-equal-cards.

Alternating Rows

feat-alternating

Light alternating left/right rows pairing copy with a distinct CSS-built visual each time. Generous vertical rhythm. Best for products with 2-4 genuinely distinct capabilities worth explaining in depth.

Stat Band

feat-stat-band

Dark horizontal band of 3-4 large, specific metrics with short context labels — high contrast, minimal, drops in cleanly between sections to reinforce credibility mid-page.

Icon Trio (done right)

feat-icon-trio

Three-column icon cards done with genuine craft — distinctive inline-SVG icons in tinted boxes, varied copy weight, subtle lift on hover. Use sparingly and only when the three things genuinely deserve equal billing.

Two-Column Detail List

feat-two-col

Dark two-column layout pairing a sticky headline/intro with a vertical list of detailed feature rows, each with icon, title, and description. Good for feature-dense B2B products.

Pricing Sections 4

Classic Three Column

price-three-col

Light three-tier pricing with monthly/annual toggle, middle tier visually elevated with "Most popular" badge, check+x feature lists, and FAQ accordion below. The complete, conversion-ready pattern.

Dark Featured Glow

price-dark-featured

Dark-background pricing with the recommended tier wrapped in a soft accent glow ring — feels premium and product-forward. Two-tier focus rather than three for simpler offerings.

Minimal Two-Tier

price-minimal

Ultra-clean two-card pricing with generous whitespace and no visual noise — best for simple products with a straightforward free-vs-paid decision.

Comparison Table

price-comparison

Feature-by-feature comparison table across three tiers with grouped categories — best for products where buyers need to scan capabilities side by side before deciding.

Testimonials 3

Card Grid

test-card-grid

Light 3-column testimonial cards with star ratings, specific outcome-focused quotes, and full name+role+company. Varied card heights avoid the masonry-template look.

Large Featured Quote

test-large-quote

Dark section with a single large, featured pull-quote and attribution — high impact, minimal, ideal for placing one exceptional testimonial front and center.

Auto-Scroll Marquee

test-marquee

Light section with two rows of compact testimonial cards auto-scrolling in opposite directions — creates a sense of momentum and abundance of social proof without taking much vertical space.

cta-full-width

Full-width dark gradient band with a bold closing headline and prominent CTA — designed to be the final word before the footer.

Minimal Centered

cta-minimal

Navigation 2

Dark Blur-on-Scroll

nav-dark-blur

Fixed dark nav that gains a frosted-glass blur and bottom border once the page scrolls — one of the simplest details that separates polished sites from flat ones.

Light Clean

nav-light-clean

Sticky light nav with a subtle bottom border that strengthens on scroll. Clean, minimal, works for nearly any light-themed site.

Footers 2

Dark Multi-Column

footer-dark-multi

Full multi-column dark footer — logo+tagline, grouped link columns, social icons, copyright row. The complete pattern for content-rich sites.

Light Minimal

footer-light-minimal

Simple light footer — single row with logo, a few links, and copyright. Best for waitlist pages, portfolios, and simpler sites that don't need a sprawling sitemap.

accent-logo-cloud

Auto-scrolling company logo strip with edge fade masks. Place directly below the hero or above features.

How It Works — 3 Steps

accent-how-it-works

Numbered steps with dashed connector — the "how it works" section every landing page needs. AI usually writes a bullet list; this looks designed.

FAQ Accordion

accent-faq

Smooth max-height expand/collapse with rotating + icon. Standalone — not tied to pricing, place anywhere for objection handling.

Live Activity Toast

accent-live-toast

Tab Feature Switcher

accent-tab-features

Pill tabs switching between 3 feature areas, each with unique copy and a CSS product visual. Far more engaging than static feature cards for SaaS products.

Trust & Security Badges

accent-trust-badges

SOC 2, GDPR, uptime, encryption row. Instantly boosts credibility for B2B and fintech. Place before or after pricing.

Animated Counter Stats

accent-counter-stats

World Reach Map

accent-world-map

SVG dot-grid world map with animated curved connection arcs that draw in on load. High visual drama for "used in X countries" positioning.

Product Dashboard Mockup

CSS browser window framing a fake product dashboard — sidebar, stat cards, bar chart. A "screenshot" with no image.

Alternating Feature Rows

accent-feature-rows

Two alternating text/visual rows with layered card compositions. More considered than three equal icon-cards.

Competitor Comparison Table

accent-comparison-table

"Us vs. others" table with a highlighted winning column. Strong conversion driver right before pricing.

Vertical Milestone Timeline

accent-timeline

Connected-line timeline of milestones with glowing accent dots. Strong for "our story" sections or roadmap narratives.

accent-bento-grid

Video Walkthrough Frame
